# Surface brief — site/index.html (VARSA static explorer)

## Scope and visitor mode

The public static explorer (Vercel): hero + national record + 3D atlas + method
notes. Mode: **Operate** (a data explorer) with the first viewport carrying a
Persuade-grade statement of the live fact. Code-led build.

## Audience, job, proof, constraints

- Faculty review panel, statistics students, general public; laptop + projector,
  phones for the public link.
- Job: read the state of the Indian monsoon instantly (live 2026 reading), then
  explore 125 years and the 3D atlas.
- Proof: every figure comes from the validated observed/pipeline layers; the
  departure colour semantics and provenance tagging are binding.
- Constraints: keep all working functionality (deck.gl atlas, season/year
  controls, play-through, national series toggle, tooltips); WCAG AA; the fixed
  departure palette does real data jobs.

## Direction contract (locked: The Survey Sheet — seed:52f2ecd5b0cac593)

- **THESIS:** The monsoon record drawn as one survey sheet — everything
  registers to a graticule rail, the departure ribbon is the mapped terrain,
  and the live 2026 reading is the sheet's newest benchmark. Refuses the
  SaaS arrangement (eyebrow + gradient hero + identical KPI cards).
- **OWN-WORLD:** Stark plate-white paper (#FBF8F1), ink black (#1C1917),
  publication salmon-red (#C0532E), survey blue (#2E5E6E) — two-colour
  lithography plus data inks. Bricolage Grotesque display (self-hosted),
  Public Sans body, Spline Sans Mono for figures. Hairline rules, engraved
  caps, dense ledger strip; no glass, no gradients, no emoji icons — drawn
  SVG stroke icons only.
- **STORY:** This is a surveyed, validated record; the visitor trusts each
  number's provenance, reads the 2026 deficit as the newest benchmark, and
  explores the 3D subcontinent and the 125-year series.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Header as a plain ruled bar (wordmark + standard nav);
  cartouche title block at top-left carrying the live 2026 reading
  (actual/normal/departure rows, rendered from meta.json); Plate I to its
  right — the departure ribbon owning the field, provisional year dashed and
  stamped; dense five-cell ledger strip along the bottom edge. Primary action:
  scroll to the atlas.
- **FORM:** Assigned direction (candidate 5 of the grounded list).
  SEED REFERENCE: seed:52f2ecd5b0cac593.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with
  the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying
