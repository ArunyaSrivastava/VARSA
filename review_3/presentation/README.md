# Review 3 — Presentation Materials

**Stage:** Review 3 (final report, 10 marks)

---

## 1. Slide Outline (suggested)

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title slide (project name, your name, date) |
| 2 | Research question & why it matters |
| 3 | Scope & boundaries |
| 4 | Data sources & schema (summary) |
| 5 | Methods (statistical / analysis) |
| 6 | Key findings (2–4 figures) |
| 7 | Feasibility verdict & risks |
| 8 | Conclusion & recommendation |
| 9 | Q&A / next steps |

---

## 2. Slide Guidelines

- Max 1 idea per slide
- 24pt+ font, 30–40pt for titles
- Figures: high resolution, labeled, no clutter
- Text: 20% text, 80% figures/visuals
- Practice timing: ~1 min per slide
